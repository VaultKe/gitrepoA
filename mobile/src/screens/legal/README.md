# VaultKe Legal Documentation

This directory contains the legal documentation screens for the VaultKe mobile application, designed to comply with Kenyan law and financial regulations.

## Files

### 1. PrivacyPolicyScreen.js
Comprehensive privacy policy covering:
- Data collection and usage
- Compliance with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019
- User rights and data protection measures
- International data transfers
- Contact information for data protection inquiries

### 2. TermsOfServiceScreen.js
Detailed terms of service including:
- Service description and eligibility
- Financial regulations compliance (CBK)
- User responsibilities and prohibited activities
- Fees, charges, and loan terms
- Dispute resolution mechanisms
- Termination procedures

## Legal Compliance

### Kenyan Laws Addressed:
- **Data Protection Act, 2019**: Privacy rights and data processing
- **Central Bank of Kenya (CBK) Regulations**: Financial services compliance
- **Consumer Protection Act**: Fair treatment and transparency
- **Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Guidelines**: KYC and reporting requirements

### Key Features:
- Clear, understandable language
- Comprehensive coverage of user rights
- Regulatory compliance information
- Contact details for support and complaints
- Regular update mechanisms

## Integration

These screens are integrated into the authentication flow through:
1. **AuthTabNavigator**: Navigation routing
2. **RegisterScreen**: Enhanced legal agreement section
3. **LegalAgreementSection**: Interactive component with key points

## Usage

Users must read and accept both documents during registration. The enhanced legal agreement section provides:
- Quick overview of key protections
- Direct links to full documents
- Clear consent mechanism
- Error handling for incomplete acceptance

## Updates

Legal documents should be reviewed and updated:
- When regulations change
- At least annually
- When new features are added
- Upon legal counsel recommendation
